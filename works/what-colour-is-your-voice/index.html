What Colour Is Your Voice?

Voice translated into colour and movement.

2023 / Audio-visual study

Project

An early experiment connecting sound, image and subjective perception.

What Colour Is Your Voice? uses vocal input to change colour and moving image in real time. Speaking or singing activates a TouchDesigner-based visual system, bringing voice, emotion and self-expression into an interactive audio-visual work.
