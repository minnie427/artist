Meta Rose / Suspended living-rose installation

A spatial presentation of Meta Rose using suspended living roses within an interactive audio-visual environment.

Exhibition context

Meta Rose was presented in the Bugskin Chapter 2 group exhibition at Norla Dome in Docklands, Melbourne, in November 2025. The installation adapted the rose interfaces and their spatial arrangement to the group exhibition setting.

Suspended rose interfaces

Living roses were suspended within Meta Rose’s interactive environment of sound and moving image. Their hanging arrangement brought the material presence of the flowers into the exhibition space, extending the work’s tactile interfaces through a configuration specific to this presentation.
