Live Performance

Sound and moving image composed in real time.

2023–2024 / Live audio-visual performance

Median / Median 2

Miscellania, Melbourne

A live practice developed through Median and Median 2, bringing original sound and real-time moving image into a shared composition.

Real-time composition

Park’s live performances explore the relationship between sound, image and the duration of performance. Original sound and real-time moving image are composed in relation to the atmosphere and contingencies of the live setting. Median and Median 2 are distinct presentations within this developing practice.

Median 2 / 2024

June and October 2024 · Miscellania, Melbourne. Median 2 developed Park’s approach to real-time composition, bringing original sound and moving image into direct relation with the duration, atmosphere and contingencies of live performance.

Median / 2023

November 2023 · Miscellania, Melbourne. Median established an early framework for Park’s live practice, placing original sound and real-time moving image in direct relation with the duration and atmosphere of performance. It formed an early study of sound and image as materials composed within the time of a live event.
