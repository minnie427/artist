Live Performance

Sound and moving image composed in real time.

2023—2024 / Median / Median 2

Miscellania, Melbourne

Project

A live practice developed through Median and Median 2.

Park’s live performances bring original sound and real-time moving image into a shared composition. Presented through Median in 2023 and Median 2 in 2024, these works explore the relationship between sound, image and the duration of performance.
