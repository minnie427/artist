Practice as shared learning

Park’s workshops extend her installation practice into shared learning. Participants explore how touch, gesture and real-time systems can become materials for making, through practical exercises and collective experimentation. The programmes connect the sensory and participatory questions of her art with hands-on approaches to creative technology.

Touching Resonance

15 April 2026 · Seventh Gallery, Melbourne · Participatory audio-visual workshop led by Minnie Park. Presented as part of Seventh Gallery’s public programme, Touching Resonance was developed and led by Park in her individual capacity as an artist.

Participants activated sound and real-time visuals collectively through conductive touch mediated by flowers and plants, as well as hand-to-hand contact. The workshop carried Park’s research into affect and participation beyond the exhibition format, testing how a tactile system can support collective making in a workshop setting.

Artists, Machines and the Space Between

October 2025 · Science Gallery Melbourne · Artist-led creative technology workshop presented with TouchCollective. The workshop explored MediaPipe as a performative tool through gesture control, hand tracking and object detection.

The programme introduced artists to body-led creative technology while creating space for shared experimentation. Gesture and computer vision were approached as tools for making and performance, connecting technical exploration with the participant’s physical presence and movement.

TouchCollective

Park is a co-founder of TouchCollective, an artist-led community centred on TouchDesigner and creative practice. Through workshops and tutorials, the collective provides a setting for artists to exchange technical knowledge, develop interactive work and experiment together.

Her contribution connects expertise in TouchDesigner with her work in tactile interfaces, sound and real-time image. As the community expands in Melbourne, she continues to develop workshops and regular online tutorials.
