Minnie Park

INTERACTIVE AUDIO-VISUAL ARTIST
SEOUL / MELBOURNE

An interactive space for emotional ambivalence.

Works ↗
?

Each feeling leaves a different trace. Your selections accumulate until you reset the rose.

Camera is optional. Your face moves the rose; use your palm to hold a word, then pinch to select it. Face and room video stay hidden and are processed in your browser without recording or uploading. No microphone is requested. Tracking files load only when you enable the camera, which stops when you leave the tab. Feelings are selected by you, not inferred from your face.

The rose is an image-derived relief with limited rotation. Its responses are artistic compositions, not emotional assessments.

Exploring the coexistence of conflicting feelings through pink, roses and interactive audio-visual installations.

Touch, sound and moving image open a space to encounter emotional ambivalence.
