Camera & privacy

Camera is optional. Face position moves the rose; your palm holds and selects a word. Your face and room stay hidden. Video is processed in this browser without recording or uploading, and no microphone is requested. Tracking files download only when you enable the camera. Camera stops when you leave this tab. Words are chosen by you, not inferred from your face. Effects are compositional responses, not an emotional assessment. Text depth is a simulated field; rose-region positions and velocities are projected from sampled image geometry. This is an image-derived relief with limited rotation, not a complete 360-degree rose model.

Exploring affect, colour, touch, and culturally situated emotional experience through immersive digital installations.

Through roses, water, sound and moving image, touch becomes a way of entering complex feeling.
